California requires a contractor's licence for any landscaping job over $500 in labour and materials. Plenty of yard crews do not hold one — and if an uninsured worker is hurt on your property, that can become your problem.

  • Active C-27 licence — held since 2023, the classification specific to landscaping.
  • Workers' compensation insurance carried for our crew — many yard services carry none.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with Merchants Bonding Company, on file with the state.
  • Free estimates — you know the price before we start.
